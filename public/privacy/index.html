Privacy Policy

Lomed TV · מסך הלמידה — app and website · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Lomed TV turns a text you bring — pasted text, a file, a photographed page or a recorded lesson — into chapters with study tools. This page explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete it. The app is operated by Haim Amar, an independent developer in Israel. Contact: haimamar135@gmail.com.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Camera, microphone and photos

  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to photograph or upload a page. The image is sent to the text-recognition service to be read, and is not stored on our servers.
  • Microphone is used only when you record a lesson, dictate, or join a video study session. A recorded lesson is sent for transcription; the audio file itself stays on your device.
  • Video study sessions are live and are not recorded by us.

Shared study sessions

When you open a shared session, the people you invite can see the page you share, your display name, and the highlights and notes you make on that page during the session.

Deleting your data

In the app: open the cloud account window (the ☁ button) and choose “Delete account and all data”. This permanently deletes your account, your synced library, your notes and results, and your generated audio files from our servers, and clears the app's data on that device.

By email: write to haimamar135@gmail.com from the address of your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

You can also delete any single book from your library at any time; its text, notes and results are removed from our servers as well.

Retention and security

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and each user can access only their own records.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
